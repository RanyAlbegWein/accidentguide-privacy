# Privacy Policy for AccidentGuide

**Last Updated:** December 6, 2025

## Introduction

AccidentGuide ("we," "our," or "the app") is a mobile application designed to help users document car accidents. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

**Key Point:** All your data stays on your device. We do not collect, store, or have access to any of your personal information on our servers.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

- **Personal Details:** Name, phone number, and insurance provider (optional, entered in your profile)
- **Document Photos:** Photos of your driver's license and insurance card (stored locally for quick sharing during an accident)
- **Vehicle Information:** Vehicle names and license plate numbers

### Information Collected During Accident Documentation

When you use the app to document an accident, the following information is collected and stored **on your device only**:

- **Location Data:** GPS coordinates and address of the accident location
- **Photos and Videos:** Images and videos you capture of the accident scene, vehicle damage, and other relevant documentation
- **Audio Recordings:** Voice notes you record describing the incident
- **Other Driver Information:** Photos of the other driver's license and insurance documents that you capture
- **Event Details:** Name/description you assign to the accident report, timestamps

## How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to:

1. **Document accidents** - Store photos, videos, location, and notes for your records
2. **Enable sharing** - Allow you to share reports with insurance companies, law enforcement, or other parties when you choose
3. **Display maps** - Show your accident location on a map within the app
4. **Provide emergency access** - Display regional emergency phone numbers based on your location

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage Only

- **All data is stored locally on your device** in the app's private storage
- **We do not operate servers** that collect or store your data
- **We cannot access your data** - it never leaves your device unless you explicitly share it

### Data Security

- App data is stored in Android's secure app-specific storage
- Data is protected by your device's security (PIN, fingerprint, etc.)
- Screenshots are blocked when viewing sensitive photos within the app

## Data Sharing

### When You Share

You have full control over sharing. Data is only shared when you explicitly:

- Tap the "Share" button on a report
- Use the "Share with Other Driver" feature in your profile

When you share, data goes **directly from your device** to the recipient via Android's share system (email, messaging apps, etc.). We are not involved in this transfer.

### Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

| Service | Purpose | Data They Receive |
|---------|---------|-------------------|
| Google Maps SDK | Display maps and location | Map tile requests, approximate location for map display |
| Google Play Billing | Process premium purchases | Payment information (handled entirely by Google) |

We do not use:
- Analytics services
- Advertising networks
- Crash reporting services that collect personal data
- Social media SDKs

## Permissions We Request

| Permission | Why We Need It |
|------------|----------------|
| **Location** | To record where the accident occurred and show relevant emergency numbers |
| **Camera** | To take photos and videos of the accident scene and documents |
| **Microphone** | To record audio notes describing what happened |

All permissions are used solely for the stated purposes. You can revoke permissions at any time in your device settings.

## Data Retention

- **Your data remains on your device** until you delete it
- Delete individual reports anytime within the app
- Uninstalling the app removes all associated data
- We have no data to retain because we never receive it

## Children's Privacy

AccidentGuide is not intended for use by children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control:

- **Access:** View all your data anytime in the app
- **Delete:** Remove any report or your entire profile at any time
- **Export:** Share/export your reports whenever you choose
- **Portability:** Your data is in standard formats (images, audio files)

## Premium Features

AccidentGuide offers optional premium features through a one-time purchase:

- Payment is processed entirely by Google Play
- We receive confirmation of purchase status only (no payment details)
- Your premium status is stored locally on your device

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

- Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top
- Posting the new policy in the app and on our website
